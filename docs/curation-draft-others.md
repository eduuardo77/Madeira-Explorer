# Draft for the other four categories — a list to veto, not to approve

**For:** the project lead. **Written:** 2026-09-22. **Method:** D-064 draft-and-veto, under D-078.
Marks as in [`curation-draft.md`](curation-draft.md); `[wiki N]` is the number of Wikipedia
languages with an article (fame, not merit).

**Why this list differs from August's:** August started from `pois.candidates.json`, which is
built from the tile pack's *labels*, so it never contained the Botanical Garden, the Sé, the market
or the Ponta do Pargo lighthouse. This one queried OSM directly (616 named attractions, viewpoints,
beaches and villages on the main island) and measured each one's distance from the pack's
existing geofences. **Anything inside an existing stamp's circle was dropped**, which is why
Cristo Rei, the Monte toboggans, the Zona Velha and Praia da Alagoa aren't proposed: you'd collect
them already.

## Two corrections to places already in the pack

- ⚠ **Encumeada Baixa**: August's reason for keeping it was *"the pass where the island's two
  coasts meet"*. **That describes the Boca da Encumeada, and this entry is 8 km away**, on the
  Pico Ruivo ridge. So the reason was wrong. I'm proposing the real pass below. Whether
  Encumeada Baixa still earns a stamp on its own is your call; I have nothing else to defend it
  with.
- ⚠ **Pico Guindaste** (Faial): OSM has a viewpoint by that name exactly where the stamp is, and
  two viewpoints named *Guindaste* **470 m east**, outside the stamp's 250 m circle. If the
  glass-floored miradouro people drive to is the eastern one `[knowledge]`, the stamp is in the
  wrong place. **Question: which one is it?**
