## The eight

### Beaches (2)

1. **Praia do Porto Santo**: the 9 km of golden sand the island is known for, and why most
   people take the ferry `[knowledge]`. The stamp should sit on the beach away from town (the town
   end is 550 m from Vila Baleira's stamp), or have several circles along it so any stretch earns
   it.
2. ⚠ **Ponta da Calheta**: the beach's southwest end, rocks and the view across to Ilhéu de Baixo,
   the island's sunset spot `[osm beach]` `[knowledge]`. **Unsure:** it is the same beach, 9 km
   on; a second beach stamp for one beach may read as padding. If you cut it, Zimbralinho or Pico
   Branco below can take the place.

### Viewpoints (3)

3. **Pico do Facho**: the highest point, 516 m `[osm peak, ele 516]` `[wiki 6]`, named for the fire
   beacons lit there to warn of pirates `[knowledge]`. The whole island from one place.
4. **Pico do Castelo**: 437 m `[osm peak]` `[wiki 6]`, the ruins of the fort the islanders fled to
   from pirates `[osm ruins: Abrigo do Pico do Castelo]`, a planted pine wood and the view down over
   Vila Baleira and the beach. 1.2 km from Facho, so the two circles do not overlap.
5. **Miradouro da Portela**: the windmills and the classic view of the whole beach curving away
   `[osm viewpoint]` `[knowledge]`. The postcard shot, and on the road in from the harbour.

### Village (1)

6. **Vila Baleira**: the island's only town: the square, the church, and Casa Colombo, the house
   where Columbus is said to have lived after marrying the governor's daughter `[osm museum]`
   `[wiki 2]`. One stamp covers the museum, the Forte de São José and the pier: all inside it.

### Landmarks (2)

7. **Pico de Ana Ferreira**: columns of basalt standing like organ pipes in an old quarry, a
   classified geosite `[osm attraction: Geossítio Pico de Ana Ferreira]` `[wiki 3]`. The strangest
   thing on the island, and a short stop.
8. ⚠ **Fonte da Areia**: sandstone cliffs on the north coast carved by the wind, and the spring
   with a legend of youth `[osm attraction, viewpoint]` `[knowledge]`. **Unsure:** the spring is
   small; the draw is the carved rock and the wild coast. A steep path down.

## Left out, and why (swap any in)

- **Zimbralinho**: a small cove with clear water for snorkelling `[osm beach]`. ⚠ `[knowledge]`:
  I believe its footpath has been closed for rockfall at times. If it is open, it is a strong
  replacement for Ponta da Calheta.
- **Miradouro das Flores**: the southwest viewpoint over Ilhéu de Baixo `[osm viewpoint]`. 500 m
  from Ponta da Calheta, so the two circles would nearly touch: one or the other.
- **Pico Branco and Terra Chã**: the island's best walk (PR 1), the wild north east cliffs
  `[osm peak, 450 m]` `[knowledge]`. A hike, not a stop: closest in spirit to a levada. Would be a
  viewpoint stamp at Terra Chã.
- **Casa Colombo** on its own: 200 m from the town's centre, so Vila Baleira's stamp already takes it.
- **Forte de São José, Pontão das Salinas**: inside the town's circle too.
