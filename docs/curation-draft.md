# Curation draft — a list to veto, not to approve

**For:** the project lead. **Written:** 2026-08-16. **Method:** D-064 (greatest hits, no filler;
the assistant drafts and the project lead vetoes).

**What to do with this:** go down it deleting. Everything here is a proposal, and the ones marked
⚠ are the ones I am least sure about — start there. **If most of it comes back deleted, D-064's
own reversal clause applies and we go back to me preparing and you selecting.**

## How each call was made, and how much to trust it

Every line carries the basis for its reason:

| Mark | Basis | How much to trust it |
|---|---|---|
| `[osm]` | A tag in OpenStreetMap — elevation, fort, fee, a PR route number | Checkable, and checked |
| `[wiki N]` | N language Wikipedias have an article | A proxy for *famous*, not for *good* |
| `[knowledge]` | **My own impression of the place, from training data. Not verified.** | **This is where I am most likely to be wrong, and you have actually been there** |
| `[thin]` | Almost no evidence either way | A question for you, not a claim by me |

⚠ **The single most important thing on this page:** roughly a third of the calls below rest on
`[knowledge]` alone. That is unverifiable from this repository, it is exactly the kind of fluent
guess D-064 warns about, and it is the reason this is a veto sheet rather than a commit.

## The three things the evidence found on its own

1. **Pico Ruivo is missing.** 1,862 m, the highest point on the island, thirty language
   Wikipedias — more than any viewpoint currently in the pack. It is absent because the tile pack
   labels it `Ruivo` and the fragment never got matched. **The Areeiro–Ruivo vereda is arguably
   the island's signature walk, and neither end of it is collectable today.**
2. **Seven of the 21 viewpoints are roadside pull-ins named "Pico something"** with no elevation,
   no article and no tag beyond `viewpoint`. They are in the pack because the tile pack labels
   them, which is prominence, not merit.
3. **Machico is missing** — 36 language Wikipedias, the island's second town, where Zarco landed —
   while Canhas and Campanário, which are parishes you drive through, are in.

## What happens after you veto

1. You strike out whatever you disagree with — including whole sections if the judgement is off.
2. I fetch coordinates and radii from OSM for whatever survives on the **add** list, and re-run
   the four checks (names, boundaries, offshore, duplicates).
3. **Then the levada endpoints**, separately — I propose two ends per levada from OSM parking,
   route markers and where the path meets a road, and you correct them. That is the pass where a
   wrong answer produces a stamp nobody can earn, so it is worth doing on its own.
