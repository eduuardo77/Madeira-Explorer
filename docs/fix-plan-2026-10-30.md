## E3 in detail: tests for the profile switching glue

Keep the convention: each module splits into a pure half (decisions, tested) and a thin wrapper
(calls Expo, the database, the clock).

- `samplingGate.ts` (273 lines): pull out *given the stored profile, the new batch, the clock and
  the outing state, which options to apply*.
- `tripRecording.ts` (227), `backgroundTasks.ts` (279): pull out the batch handler's decisions
  (which profile, store or drop, whether to re-arm the motion wake).
- `walkSession.ts` (198), `recorderHealth.ts` (196): pull out the state transitions and the health
  verdicts.
- A small replay harness: fake provider, fake clock, in-memory store; it feeds a batch sequence
  through the pure halves in order and records each profile change.
- **Regression cases from the real database copies:** 26 Oct 17:49 to 18:18, still on *driving*
  with no fix through (in `p30-2026-10-27-before-n15`); 27 Oct restart and the profile cache race
  (`before-n15-race`); 30 Oct 16:15 and 19:25 leftover batches and the still check 91 min late
  (`before-t294`, `after-outing`). Batches cut to the minutes that matter.
- **How we know:** each case fails when its fix commit is reverted (`e7e2ac6`, `1220603`, the
  T-286 change), the way `traceDrawn.test.ts` was checked.

app/src/screens/NativeMapScreen.tsx app/patches`. Suspects: T-282's pin image patch (25 Oct) and
`db2441c` (the blue dot made conditional). Four marked runs per step.
