## ⚠ Judgement calls for you

1. **The times do not say one way or return.** PR6 is 4,3 km one way and "3h00", which fits a
   return walk; PR10 is 11 km one way and "5h". Showing *4,3 km · 3h00* would mislead. My
   proposal: show the distance with *ida e volta* where the page gives a return figure, and the time
   beside it, as the page pairs them. Strike it if you would rather show distance only.
2. **Two stamps point to closed trails** (PR7, PR28). The app works offline and cannot know a
   closure the day it happens. Options: (a) keep the stamps and show on every levada card
   *"Confirma o estado do percurso antes de ir"* (check the trail's status before going); (b) take
   PR7 and PR28 out of the 80 while closed (the count would change, which D-089 forbids lowering
   after release, but not before); (c) both. I recommend (a): closures come and go, and a fixed
   list cannot track them, so the honest thing is to say so on every walk.
3. **The fee, and booking.** The classified walks are paid and must be booked through the
   SIMplifica portal; Madeira's residents are exempt. The amount has changed: VisitMadeira's FAQ
   gives **4,50 € from 1 January 2026** for the general public (3 € with an operator that has an
   IFCN protocol), while an IFCN notice gives other figures again. ⚠ My first draft said "3 € since
   28 October 2024", from a search summary; that was out of date. So: no price in the app, which
   would go stale offline. At most one line on a PR card, *"Percurso pago, com reserva"* (paid,
   booking required), if you want it.
4. **Rocha Vermelha's start.** The page gives *Fanal (ER209)*; our why-go line places it in the
   Rabaçal network, below the 25 Fontes. Both may be true of different ends; worth a look.
