The rank seal

The wax seal, developed. It borrows its vocabulary from the stamps it counts — perforation beading and a sunburst are already stampArt.ts's own devices — so the medal is the same family of object as the thing it is a medal for, rather than a trophy imported from somewhere else.

⚠ Exclusivity is not brightness. A brighter seal is just a paler seal. Two things carry it instead: the ornament accumulates (nothing → beading → rays → laurel → faceted rim and double ring), and the material changes at platinum — bronze, silver and gold are wax, with a poured edge and a broad sheen; platinum is not. Its edge is crisp, its highlight is a hard cold sliver and its rim is cut into facets. It stops being the same substance.

⚠ No SVG filters. The cast shadow is an offset copy, the deboss is three passes, the sheen is a gradient — because feGaussianBlur is unreliable in react-native-svg on Android and this has to be drawable by the app.

Side by side — what changed

⚠ Top row is the edition you chose, untouched. Bottom row is the refinement. Judging a change against a description is how round 5 slipped through; judging it against the thing it replaced is harder to fool.

Nothing was added. Every difference is light or material: an occlusion ring where the well meets the face, a machined lip at the rim, a second tighter shadow for contact, a bounce arc on the unlit side, a deeper press for the mark — and a per-rank falloff, so platinum's face turns over like a mirror and bronze's like something matte. Same hues throughout.
