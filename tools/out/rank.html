The rank seal — five vocabularies

The last one was a poker chip and the project lead named exactly why: "os pontos na volta e as linhas do centro para fora". Evenly spaced dots around a rim plus radial spokes from the centre is not like a casino chip, it is one. Both devices were mine and both are gone.

⚠ Worth remembering how they got there: they were borrowed from stampArt.ts's own perforation and sunburst, on the reasoning that the medal should share the stamps' vocabulary. The reasoning was sound and the result was still wrong — a good rule applied without looking produced a casino chip.

Each variant is shown at study size and then at the real 64 dp button. ⚠ The study size flatters everything.

1 — Cord and laurel

A twisted rope border and sprigs that grow with rank. Nothing evenly spaced, nothing radiating — the two things that made a chip.

2 — Lettered rim

The app's name and the rank set around the edge, engraved. What a real seal or coin does, and text is the one thing a poker chip never has.

3 — Guilloché

The engine-turned wave from banknotes and watch dials. Closed rings, so it cannot read as spokes however dense it gets.

4 — Pure wax

No geometry at all. A poured edge, a mottled surface, a drip. Rank carried entirely by material and by how rich the pour is. ⚠ The furthest from a game and the closest to a passport.

5 — The mark is the seal

The outline is the app's stamp mark rather than a disc containing it — which removes the "disc with a thing inside" reading at the root.

All five at button size, on green

⚠ Most of this island is laurel forest. Gold on green is the real test, not gold on a neutral panel. Gold and platinum only, since those are the ones that have to carry the feeling.

Greyscale — with the colour taken away

⚠ The test that matters most. If a variant's ranks are only distinguishable by hue, then a colour-blind user has one rank and everybody else has five.
